Method

Data. Studies whose lead sponsor is one of the seven institutions (or an affiliated entity) were pulled from ClinicalTrials.gov, then cleaned and flattened in a Python notebook into one row per trial (status, phase, enrollment, intervention types, conditions, industry collaborators). The same tables power the Tableau workbook and this page.

Therapeutic areas are assigned from MeSH condition terms first, then keyword rules on condition text; trials matching neither are "Other / unclassified". Industry-partnered means at least one listed collaborator is a company. Stopped early covers terminated, withdrawn and suspended studies.

Caveats. Registry data is self-reported and lags; some start dates are anticipated or mis-entered; and lead-sponsor filtering undercounts trials where an institution is a participating site rather than the sponsor.
