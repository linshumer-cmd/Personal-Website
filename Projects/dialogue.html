Multicellular Programs
in Liver Cancer

Applying DIALOGUE to a liver cancer single-cell RNA-seq cohort to find multicellular programs: sets of genes in different cell types that rise and fall together across patients, pointing to coordinated cell–cell interactions in the tumor microenvironment.

Still working on this one.

Results and write-up will be posted here as the analysis matures.

Questions

  • Do those multicellular programs line up with clinical features or outcomes?
  • How well do results reproduce when the method is re-run independently on the same data?
